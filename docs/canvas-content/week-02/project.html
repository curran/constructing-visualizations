Create a new Markdown file in your course repository called `docs/FINAL_PROJECT_EXPLORATION_1.md` sketching out some high level ideas for your final project. Include reference links to potential datasets you may want to try using, and links to related existing work.

Use this document to explore some high-level ideas for your final project. You are not expected to decide on a final topic or visualization yet. The goal of this exercise is to generate possibilities that you can refine over the next several weeks.

In your document, include:

  • A topic or domain that you are interested in exploring.
  • A few questions you might want to investigate through data visualization.
  • Links to one or more potential datasets, or descriptions of the kinds of data you would like to find.
  • Links to related visualizations, articles, projects, or other existing work that could serve as inspiration.
  • Several rough sketches of possible visualization ideas. These should be drawn by hand on paper. Take photographs or scans of your sketches, add them to your repository, and include them in the Markdown document. Tip: you can paste the image while editing Markdown in GitHub, and it will auto-upload for you. Alternatively, add the images files to the repo and embed them in the Markdown.
  • A few sentences describing what you were thinking about in each sketch.

Do not spend much time making the sketches polished or visually attractive. They are meant to be quick, disposable ideas. Try different approaches rather than attempting to find the "right" visualization.

At this stage, breadth is more important than commitment. You will revisit and narrow these ideas in future project exploration assignments.

By the end of the course, your project should be an interactive data visualization application where users can explore and interact with the data. The goal is to have a professional level open source portfolio piece. The format options are pretty much:
  • interactive dashboard
  • scrollytelling dynamic visualization
  • some type of app that is based around visualizations
  • some combination of those

You can also model the ideas off existing interactive visualizations you've seen. Just put some ideas on the page, let them become clear later.
